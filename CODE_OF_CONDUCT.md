# Code of conduct

AwesomeFigures is a scientific software project. Contributors, users,
reviewers and maintainers are expected to communicate constructively in
issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-associated events.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional toward participants with different
  backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.
- Explain technical disagreements with reproducible evidence and clear
  reasoning. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Credit prior work, authors, datasets, software, and third-party assets.
- Respect privacy and avoid disclosing confidential or identifying material.
- Welcome constructive feedback and make room for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks,
sexualized misconduct, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private
information without consent are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary project-process concerns, contact repository maintainers through
GitHub issues without sharing sensitive personal details. For confidential
misconduct concerns, request a **private contact channel** from an organization
maintainer without describing the allegation publicly. If the concern involves
a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, request changes in
behavior, moderate discussions, or restrict participation. They will aim to
consider available evidence and confidentiality, but cannot guarantee
anonymity on third-party platforms. Reports will be evaluated case by case.

This policy applies to AwesomeFigures-controlled project spaces and
behavior directly connected to participation in the project.
